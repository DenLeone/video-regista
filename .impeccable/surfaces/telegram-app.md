# Regista PWA

Mode: operate

Audience: Denis, on his iPhone, often on mobile data. Job: upload rushes, start a montage with an explicit tap, follow the job, approve or correct the cut.

Constraints: Italian copy; every existing action and the R2 multipart upload stay; glass only on the floating layer; solid graphite if blur fails; no automatic montage.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The phone is the remote for one job — rushes go up from anywhere, and a montage starts only when Denis taps Avvia montaggio. The screen refuses the old five-tab glyph bar and the nine-step wizard.

OWN-WORLD: Black ground, graphite cards, white as the only action color. Glass is the dock pill, the sheets, the toast, and the correction composer; content is an opaque fill plus a hairline. The signature is a seven-segment film rail: done is solid white, the current segment carries a light sweep, the rest stays quiet.

STORY: If a job is running, its phase name is the first thing he reads, with elapsed time and the rail. If nothing is running, the upload card is the hero. Video is a poster grid. Settings are grouped rows. Correction sits in a docked field.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky solid-black title “Oggi” with a Dati/Wi-Fi chip on the right. Under it, either the live job card (phase at 28/34, rail, one glass Dettagli) or a 220-tall upload card. A floating glass pill holds Oggi, Video, Altro; a white 64 pt Carica orb sits to its right, in the thumb zone.

FORM: Brief-pinned Vetro Nero. iOS navigation (tab pill, sheet, white primary button) — the world supplies palette, density, system type, and the rail, not a costume of another interface.
